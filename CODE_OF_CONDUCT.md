# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a place where anyone can take part without being harassed, whatever their background, identity or level of experience.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are doing this in their spare time, and for many English is a second language.
- Talk about the work, not the person. Say what you would change and why.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no to a change. Reasons will be given.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks, or mocking someone's work or questions.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, including unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Continuing after you have been asked to stop.

## Where this applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere else you are representing the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience something that breaks these rules, write to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting on this repository (Security, then Report a vulnerability, and say it is a conduct report). Reports are read by the maintainers only and kept private.

A maintainer who receives a report will read it, ask the people involved if needed, and decide what to do. That can be a private word, a public correction, removing a comment, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project.

This text follows the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
